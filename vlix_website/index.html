Important Note for Web App Users

We built the Vlix Web App to allow iOS users to experience our product, as Apple restrictions prevent us from offering a native app. It also serves as a way to try Vlix before downloading.

Please Note: The user experience in the web app is significantly inferior to our native apps. You may encounter annoying popups from video providers that we cannot suppress in a browser environment. Our native apps (Windows, Mac, Android) allow us to 100% suppress these ads and provide a premium, Netflix-quality experience.
